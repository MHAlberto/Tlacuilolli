# Example image credits

- **oppenheimer.jpg** shows J. Robert Oppenheimer at the Guest Lodge, Oak Ridge, in February 1946. Ed Westcott made the photograph as a U.S. government photographer. It is in the U.S. public domain. The example crops the portrait in Typst. [Rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:J._Robert_Oppenheimer_at_the_Guest_Lodge,_Oak_Ridge,_in_1946_4.jpg) · [Original JPEG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ab/J._Robert_Oppenheimer_at_the_Guest_Lodge%2C_Oak_Ridge%2C_in_1946_4.jpg)
- **los-alamos.jpg** shows Isidor Isaac Rabi, Dorothy McKibbin, J. Robert Oppenheimer, and Victor Weisskopf at Oppenheimer's Los Alamos home in 1944. The U.S. Department of Energy archive identifies the image as a U.S. government work in the U.S. public domain. [Rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HD.4G.026_(10537567494).jpg) · [Original JPEG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/58/HD.4G.026_%2810537567494%29.jpg)
- **thomson-netherfield.jpg** is Hugh Thomson's “He came down to see the place” from Jane Austen's *Pride and Prejudice*, George Allen, 1894. The edition and illustration are in the U.S. public domain. [Digitized edition](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1342/1342-h/1342-h.htm) · [Original JPEG](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1342/1342-h/images/i_031.jpg)
- **oppenheimer.pdf** and **projection.svg** are original Tlacuilolli diagrams available under the MIT license. [Editable TikZ sources](../diagrams/)

The three JPEGs are grayscale derivatives made with [prepare_image.py](../../.agents/skills/document-images/scripts/prepare_image.py). The original image dimensions and source files remain available at the links above.
